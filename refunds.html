Refunds & cancellations

Last updated: 25 November 2026

All sales are final. Because you get access to digital content and my time as soon as you join, To See As It Is does not offer refunds or cancellations on any program, course, guide or session once payment is made.

That's exactly why Coming Home starts with a 45-minute call: so we both know it's the right fit before you pay. If you have questions before buying, write to support@toseeasitis.com.

Payment problems

If you were charged twice, or money left your account but the purchase didn't go through, write to support@toseeasitis.com with your payment details. Any extra or failed payment is returned to the original payment method within 5-7 working days of confirmation.

Sessions

1:1 sessions can be rescheduled once if you tell me at least 24 hours before. A session missed without notice counts as used.
