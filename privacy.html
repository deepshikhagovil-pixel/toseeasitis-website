Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how To See As It Is ("we", "I"), run by Deepshikha Govil, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India, collects and uses your personal data when you use toseeasitis.com, book a call, buy a program, course or session, or use the members area. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What I collect

Your name, email address and phone number; your answers to booking questions; payment details needed to confirm your purchase (card and bank details are handled by the payment provider and never reach me); and, if you are a member, your course progress, check-ins, journal entries and wins you choose to write inside the members area.

Why I use it

To give you what you bought (access, calls, support), to confirm payments and send receipts, to contact you about your program, to improve the program, and, only if you agreed, to send you my emails. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time.

Who else handles it

Only trusted services that help me run this: Razorpay (payments), Calendly and Google Meet (calls), Google Workspace (email), Brevo (emails), Supabase (members area logins and data) and the video host for course lessons. I never sell your data. Anything you share on a call or in your check-ins stays between us. Your words are only ever shared as a testimonial with your permission, and never with your name unless you agree.

How long I keep it

As long as you are a member or customer, and after that only as long as the law requires (for example, for tax records).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to support@toseeasitis.com. I'll respond within 30 days. Questions or complaints go to the same address (grievance contact: Deepshikha Govil).

Cookies

The website uses what it needs to work and to keep you logged in to the members area, and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, where visits come from, device and rough location). It doesn't identify you by name.
